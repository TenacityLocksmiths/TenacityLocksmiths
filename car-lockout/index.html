How it works and response times

Call 0458 893 888 and tell us where you are, the make and model, and where the keys are. During public hours we'll give you an ETA based on your location. After hours: emergency by arrangement. We'll ask for ID before we open the car.

Public hours Monday–Friday 8:00am–5:30pm. Saturday and Sunday are closed. After hours: emergency by arrangement. We don't quote a set number of minutes; we give you an honest ETA when you call.

While you wait

  • If a child, pet or anyone else is locked in the car and could be at risk, call 000 now. Don't wait for a locksmith.
  • Stay somewhere safe and visible, away from traffic, and keep your phone charged.
  • Don't try to force a door or window; it can damage the car and the lock.

In most cases we can get you back into your car on the spot, without a tow.

Automotive only: we don't do house or commercial building locks.

Frequently asked questions

Will you damage my car?

We use non-destructive entry methods designed to open the door or boot without breaking the lock or glass.

What if a child or pet is locked in the car?

Call 000 straight away if anyone is at risk, especially on a warm day. Don't wait for a locksmith.

Can you help after hours?

After hours: emergency by arrangement. Call 0458 893 888 and we'll tell you whether we can attend.

Do you need proof the car is mine?

Yes. We check ID and that you're the owner or authorised to use the car before opening it.

What if I've lost the keys, not just locked them in?

We can open the car, then cut and program a replacement key on site for most makes and models.

Need help with car lockout?

Call or text and we'll tell you if we can reach you and when.
